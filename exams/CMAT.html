Teen Phases Mein 75 Din: Concept, Practice aur Full Mocks

25 November 2026 se 7 February 2027 tak exact 75 active study days milte hain, aur uske baad 8–10 February ka 3-day light revision buffer rehta hai taaki 11 February ko aap fresh dimaag se exam dein.

Phase 1 · 25 Nov – 29 Dec (35 Din)

Foundation & Syllabus Coverage

Har core topic ke basics aur formula sheet banayein—is phase mein speed se zyada concept clarity aur accuracy par dhyan dein.

  • Quant: Arithmetic (Percentages, Ratio, Profit & Loss, SI/CI, Work & Time, TSD, Alligation & Mixtures) complete karein.
  • LR: Number Series, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relationship, Direction & Distance, Linear Arrangements aur Venn Diagrams roz 15–20 Qs solve karein.
  • English: Roz 1 Reading Comprehension passage + Synonyms/Antonyms, Idioms & Grammar rules.
  • Mock Goal: Har dusre Sunday 1 previous year CMAT paper solve karein (total 2 papers).
Phase 2 · 30 Dec – 23 Jan (25 Din)

I&E Booster, DI Sets & Sectional Speed

Ab Innovation & Entrepreneurship aur Data Interpretation add karein, aur 30-minute timed sectional drills shuru karein.

  • I&E (Game Changer): Entrepreneurship Theories & Models, Corporate/Social/Rural/Women Entrepreneurship, Principles of Business Organisation, Motivation & Leadership revise karein.
  • Quant + DI: Probability, P&C, Geometry/Mensuration formulas, aur roz 2 DI sets (Tabulation, Line Graph, Pie/Bar Chart).
  • GA: Aug 2026 – Jan 2027 Current Affairs + Indian Constitution, Economics, Geography, History & Science in Everyday Life.
  • Mock Goal: Har week 1 full-length CBT mock + uska detailed error log (total 4 mocks).
Phase 3 · 24 Jan – 7 Feb (15 Din)

Full-Length Mocks & Accuracy Lock

Koi naya heavy topic na uthayein. Sirf full mocks, formula revision, aur −1 negative marking control karne par kaam karein.

  • Mocks: Har alternate day 3-hour full CBT mock dein (total 6–7 mocks) exam-like environment mein.
  • Analysis: Har mock ke baad 60 min sirf galat aur skipped questions par lagayein—repeated mistakes band karein.
  • Revision: Quant formula notebook, I&E concepts, aur 6-month GA one-liners daily 45 min revise karein.
  • 8–10 Feb Buffer: Koi heavy mock nahi—sirf Admit Card print, formula recap, aur poori neend.

11 February Exam Day Strategy

Kyunki CMAT mein koi sectional timer nahi hota, sahi section order choose karne se aapko last 20 minute ka review buffer aaram se mil jata hai.

  1. Pehle 45 min mein GA aur I&E niptayein: In dono sections mein ya toh answer aata hai ya nahi—fast solve karke 60–65 marks ka base pehle hi lock kar lein.
  2. Agle 70 min mein LR aur English complete karein: LR (35 min) aur LC (35 min) mein accuracy highest rakhein; kisi bhi tricky puzzle par 3 minute se zyada mat atkein.
  3. Uske baad 45 min Quant & DI ko dein: Pehle aasaan Arithmetic aur direct Table/Bar DI sets uthayein; lengthy calculation wale questions ko "Mark for Review" kar dein.
  4. Aakhri 20 min sirf Review aur Accuracy Check: Marked questions revisit karein aur blind tukke (random guesses) bilkul na lagayein—sirf tabhi educated guess lein jab aap 2 options clearly eliminate kar chuke hon (Cracku).
